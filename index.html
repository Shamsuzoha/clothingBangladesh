How an order actually moves

No factory contacts, no minimum order relationships, no guessing what's realistic to make.

1

Send us the idea

Share a design, a tech pack, or even a reference garment you like. Tell us the fabric, fit, and quantity you're thinking about, and we'll tell you what's realistic before you commit to anything.

2

We match you with a manufacturer

Your order goes to a buying house from our vetted network in Dhaka or Chattogram. We choose based on product type and quantity, not just whoever happens to have capacity that week.

3

We run production and get it to you

Sampling, fabric sourcing, and quality checks at multiple stages, then freight to your door. One point of contact from the first sample to the final shipment.

What we handle

You keep the brand, the audience, and the design decisions. We keep the production side moving.

Sourcing & factory matching

Finding and vetting the right manufacturer for your product. Fabric type, print method, and construction all factor into who gets the order.

Tech packs & sampling

Turning a design or reference garment into a manufacturable spec, then a physical sample you approve before bulk production starts.

Low-MOQ production

Running orders most factories turn away, from as few as two dozen units per style, without the usual "come back at 500" answer.

Quality control

In-line checks during production and a pre-shipment inspection, so sizing, stitching, or print issues get caught before they reach you.

Freight, duties & customs

Door-to-door shipping with import paperwork handled on our end, not left for you to figure out on arrival.

Private label & branding

Woven labels, hang tags, and packaging that make the finished product look like your brand, not a factory sample.

Why source from Bangladesh

Bangladesh's garment industry didn't happen overnight. It's the product of decades of dedicated cotton and knitwear manufacturing, a dense factory ecosystem stretching across Dhaka, Chattogram, and Gazipur, and a workforce that already produces for many of the world's largest apparel brands.

That same capability exists for a 36-piece hoodie order. It's just rarely accessible from outside the industry. Vetting factories, translating specs, chasing production updates: that's the gap we sit in, so you don't have to.

How we get paid

We don't charge a platform fee, a listing fee, or a fee to get a quote. Our commission is already built into the production price your manufacturer quotes you, so the number you see is the number you pay. No separate invoice from us.

If a factory quote changes mid-production because of a fabric substitution or spec change, you're told before it happens, not after.
