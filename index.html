Services

What we handle

You keep the brand, the audience and the design calls. We keep the production side moving.

Sourcing and factory matching

Finding and vetting the right manufacturer for your product. Fabric type, print method and construction all factor into who gets the order.

Tech packs and sampling

Turning a design or reference garment into a manufacturable spec, then a physical sample you approve before bulk production starts.

Low-MOQ production

Running orders most factories turn away, well below the usual "come back at 500" answer.

Quality control

In-line checks during production and a pre-shipment inspection, so sizing, stitching or print issues get caught before they reach you.

Freight, duties and customs

Door to door shipping with import paperwork handled on our end, not left for you to figure out on arrival.

Private label and branding

Woven labels, hang tags and packaging that make the finished product look like your brand, not a factory sample.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need my own factory contacts?+

No. That's the part we handle. You bring the design and the quantity you're thinking about, and we match you with a manufacturer suited to that product.

What's the minimum order?+

It depends on the product and fabric. Our partner factories will take on runs well below typical factory minimums, tell us what you're making and we'll confirm what's realistic.

Do you manufacture anything yourselves?+

No. Clothing Bangladesh is a sourcing and production partner, not a factory. Every order is produced by an independently owned, vetted manufacturer in the network.

How is your commission structured?+

It's built into the production quote you receive, so there's no separate platform fee. You see one number per unit, and that's what you pay.

Can you ship internationally?+

Yes, to most countries. Freight and import duties are handled by us and included in the quote, so there's nothing extra to arrange on arrival.

Ready to see what your idea would cost to make?

Send a design or a reference piece and the quantity you're considering. We'll come back with what's realistic: fabric, MOQ, timeline and price per unit.
